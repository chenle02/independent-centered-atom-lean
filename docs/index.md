# Independent Centered Atom

A Lean 4 / Mathlib library with one theorem: a bounded past-measurable coefficient times independent,
centered, square-integrable noise has conditional mean zero given the past, and its second moment
factors,

```math
\mathbb{E}[\xi Z \mid \mathcal{F}] = 0 \quad\text{a.e.},\qquad
\int (\xi Z)^2\,d\mu = V \int \xi^2\,d\mu .
```

The noise moments, independence and the bound on the coefficient are hypotheses of the Lean statement;
integrability, the conditional mean and the second moment are its conclusions.

  its hypotheses and sign-off.
- [Verification](verification.html) and [Coverage](coverage.html): pages generated by lean-pkg from the

<div class="boundary" markdown="1">
**Not proved here.** The library does not construct a Poisson random measure, a stochastic integral or
any particle model, and claims no new mathematics: this is a standard identity, packaged for reuse.
